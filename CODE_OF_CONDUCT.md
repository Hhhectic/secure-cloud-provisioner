# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume the person you are replying to is acting in good
faith and knows something you do not.

## What is expected

- Give feedback on the code, not the person who wrote it.
- Accept feedback on your own code the same way.
- Be patient with people who are new to this. Much of this project was written
  by people learning it, and the questions that look obvious are usually the
  ones worth answering properly.
- When you disagree, say what you would do instead and why.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in issues, pull requests, commit
  messages, or anywhere else connected to this project.
- Demeaning or exclusionary comments about anyone's identity, background, or
  level of experience.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussion, or deliberately derailing a thread.

## Scope

This applies anywhere the project is being worked on or represented: issues,
pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and code review.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately through the repository's
[Security tab](https://github.com/Hhhectic/secure-cloud-provisioner/security)
using **Report a vulnerability** — it is the private channel this repository has,
and it works for conduct reports as well as security ones. Reports stay
confidential.

Maintainers will look at what happened and respond in a way that fits it, which
may mean asking someone to stop, editing or removing a comment, or blocking an
account. Someone reporting in good faith will not be penalised for it, whatever
the outcome.
